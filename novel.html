Prologue

Before the Beginning

Before there were kingdoms, before there were temples, and before mankind gave names to the stars, there was a memory.

It was older than the first city and deeper than the oldest stone buried beneath the earth.

Those who carried the memory called it many things. Some called it the Beginning. Others called it the Voice. A few simply called it the Light.

But the truth had been hidden for thousands of years.

And now, it was beginning to awaken.

Chapter One

The Awakening

Maya Reed woke before dawn with the feeling that someone had spoken her name.

She sat upright in the darkness and listened.

Nothing.

Outside, the city was silent beneath the last hours of night. Yet the feeling remained.

Something was calling her.

Chapter One

The Mark

Maya reached for the ancient object resting beside her bed.

She had found it three days earlier among the belongings left behind by her grandfather.

At first she believed it was nothing more than an old piece of carved stone.

But when her fingers touched the symbol in its center, the room changed.

For a single moment, Maya saw a place she had never visited.

A mountain. A fire burning beneath a black sky. And a man standing beside an ancient gate.
