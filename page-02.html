THE AWAKENED

Book One: Before the Beginning

Prologue

The Memory

The first civilization did not believe that the world had begun with them.

Their oldest stories spoke of a time before kings, before cities, and before the temples that would eventually dominate the great river valleys.

They believed that something had come before.

A people. A presence. A knowledge that had been given to humanity and then almost completely forgotten.

The story survived because a handful of people refused to let it disappear.

They became keepers of the memory.

They traveled from city to city carrying fragments of an ancient tradition. They hid symbols inside ordinary objects and placed messages where only another keeper would know to look.

Over centuries, the message became fragmented.

One piece disappeared into the desert. Another was buried beneath a ruined temple. Another was carried north by people whose names have long since vanished from history.

Yet one final piece remained.

It had been waiting in silence for someone capable of remembering.
