The Awakened — Book One

The Voice in the Silence

— ✦ —

The night had grown strangely still.

Yehoshua remained beneath the ancient sky, watching the stars as they burned above the hills. He had seen the same heavens countless times, yet tonight they seemed different.

Closer.

Almost alive.

He lowered his eyes toward the distant valley.

Small fires flickered below, each one marking the home of a family. Somewhere in that darkness, children slept. Elders whispered prayers. Mothers prepared for another morning.

Life continued as it always had.

Yet Yehoshua could not shake the feeling that something was changing.

He closed his eyes.

At first, he heard only the wind.

Then came a voice.

Not loud.

Not distant.

It seemed to rise from somewhere deeper than sound.

Remember.

Yehoshua opened his eyes.

The stars remained above him.

The valley remained below.

But something inside him had awakened.

He looked toward the eastern horizon.

A faint light had begun to appear.

Dawn was still far away.

Yet the darkness no longer seemed complete.

For the first time, Yehoshua understood that the stories of the beginning were not merely stories about a world that had once been created.

They were warnings.

They were memories.

And perhaps they were also a promise.

Remember who you are.

Yehoshua stood silently beneath the stars.

He did not yet understand what the words meant.

But he knew one thing.

His journey had begun.
