The Awakened — Book One
Chapter Two

The Memory of the Ancients

— ✦ —

The first light of morning touched the hills.

Yehoshua had not slept.

He remained where he had stood through the night, watching the darkness slowly surrender to the rising sun.

The voice was gone.

But its words remained.

Remember who you are.

He carried those words with him as he descended toward the village.

The people were beginning their day. Doors opened. Fires were kindled. The smell of bread drifted through the morning air.

Everything appeared ordinary.

Yet Yehoshua no longer saw the world as ordinary.

The stories of creation returned to his mind.

The ancient accounts spoke of a time before kings, before nations, before temples and written books. They spoke of a beginning hidden behind the beginning everyone knew.

And somewhere within those stories was a memory humanity had forgotten.

Yehoshua stopped beside an old stone wall.

His grandfather had once told him that the oldest knowledge was rarely destroyed.

It was buried.

Hidden beneath generations of fear, war, pride, and forgetfulness.

He had thought those words were merely the wisdom of an old man.

Now he wondered whether his grandfather had known more than he had ever revealed.

A shadow passed across the ground.

Yehoshua looked upward.

For a moment, he thought he saw something moving among the clouds.

Then it disappeared.

He turned toward the house of his grandfather.

There were questions he needed answered.

And for the first time, he understood that some questions were dangerous because once asked, they could never truly be forgotten.

Yehoshua walked toward the house.

Behind him, the morning sun continued to rise.

And beneath the ordinary world, something ancient was beginning to stir.
